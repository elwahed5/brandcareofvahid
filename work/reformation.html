Magic Wardrobe. Reinventing the fitting room.

A touchscreen-enabled fitting room experience that blended the ease of online shopping with the tactility of physical stores.

No waiting. No re-dressing. Just a wardrobe that knows what you want.

Led the retail innovation strategy behind Magic Wardrobe, a best-in-class, tech-enabled fitting room experience built to kill the worst parts of trying on clothes. Stores displayed single sample pieces on the floor, and customers requested sizes, colors, and styles from an in-room touchscreen — items appeared behind the closet doors without ever flagging down an associate.

Lighting and music were also customer-controlled, turning each fitting room into a private, personalized space. As Sr. Director of Strategy & Business Operations, I also secured $25M in Series B financing and negotiated tier-1 commercial partnerships with Nordstrom, New Balance, and AWAY — helping grow revenue ~$150M, a 60% year-over-year increase.
