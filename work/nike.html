B.I.L.L. Bot Initiated Longevity Lab.

A robot-powered cleaning and repair system that extends the life of your sneakers — piloted at Nike Town London.

Retail innovation in service of a longer product life.

B.I.L.L. is one of the tier-1 in-store experiences I helped launch at Nike. It creates a 3D digital scan of each shoe, performs targeted cleaning across the upper, sidewalls, and outsole, and applies recycled polyester patches to worn areas — Nike staff finish the job with recycled liners and laces.

The system debuted at Nike Town London in September 2022, compatible with Air Force 1s, Air Jordan 1s, Space Hippie 01s, and Nike Dunks. It supports Nike's circular-economy goals alongside sibling programs Nike Refurbished and Recycling & Donation.
